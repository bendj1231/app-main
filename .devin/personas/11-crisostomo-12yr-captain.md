# Persona 11 — Crisostomo (12yr A320 Captain, bored, seniority trap)

## Profile
- **Hours**: 12,000
- **Licenses**: ATPL, A320 type rating
- **Current role**: Captain at Cebu Pacific, 12 years
- **Situation**: Bored, chained to seniority, wants change but can't leave without losing everything
- **App context**: Mid-career transition, recognition, willing to invest in the app

## 100 Questions

### The Seniority Chain — 12 Years and Trapped
1. I've been at Cebu Pacific for 12 years — if I leave for another airline, I start at the bottom of the seniority list. Is that a career or a prison sentence?
2. My seniority gives me a stable roster, good routes, and a captain's seat — but if I leave, I lose all of it. Is that loyalty or hostage-taking?
3. I want to fly long-haul, I want to fly A350s — but I'd have to start as a junior FO at another airline to do it. Why does 12,000 hours count for nothing?
4. The app mentions "Career Progression" — but what does progression look like when you're already a captain and the only way "up" is sideways?
5. I've watched junior pilots jump to Emirates and Qatar with 3,000 hours — I have 12,000 and I'd be junior to them. How is that fair?
6. Seniority is supposed to reward loyalty — but it also punishes ambition. Why is there no system that recognizes experience independent of airline tenure?
7. The app should have a "seniority calculator" — show me what I lose vs. what I gain by switching airlines. Would the numbers ever say "go"?
8. I'm 38, 12 years in, and the thought of starting over at the bottom of another airline makes me physically sick. Is that weakness or wisdom?
9. My airline knows I won't leave because of seniority — so they don't have to compete for my loyalty. Is the seniority system a cartel?
10. The app mentions "Pilot Recognition Profile" — is that the answer? A recognition system that travels with me, independent of airline seniority?

18. The app mentions "Career Pathways" — but all the pathways lead TO the airline. Where's the pathway for when you're IN the airline and want OUT?
19. I've considered downgrading to FO just to fly a different aircraft type — that's how bored I am. Is that insane?
