# Persona 12 — Captain Neeraj (15yr CFI, sim pilot, wants corporate jets)

## Profile
- **Hours**: 4,000+
- **Licenses**: CFI/CFII/MEI, IFR sim instructor qualification
- **Current role**: 15-year instructor, IFR simulator pilot
- **Situation**: Public lie ("I love teaching"), private dream (corporate jets). Trapped by the wrong hours.
- **App context**: CFI escape route, sim-to-cockpit transition, corporate pathway

## 100 Questions

### The Public Lie — "I Love Teaching"
1. I tell every student "I love teaching, this is my calling" — it's a lie I've told so many times I almost believe it. When did I start lying to myself?
2. My school uses me in their marketing — "15-year veteran instructor, passionate about shaping the next generation." I want to scream every time I see the poster.
3. Students introduce me to their parents as "the best instructor here" — I smile and say "it's my passion." When did passion become a performance?
4. I've said "teaching is its own reward" 1,000 times — if that's true, why am I miserable?
5. The app mentions "mentor network" — I'm listed as a mentor. People look up to me. And I'm drowning. Who mentors the mentor who's lying about being happy?
6. I've told 200+ students "follow your dreams" — I haven't followed mine. Is that hypocrisy or just survival?
7. When students graduate and move on to airlines, I say "I'm so proud of you" — and I am, but I'm also jealous. Is it possible to be proud and envious at the same time?
8. My instructor bio says "Captain Neeraj found his true calling in education" — I wrote that. It's fiction. When did my CV become creative writing?
9. I've been nominated for "Instructor of the Year" three times — each time I felt sick. Is an award for the cage you're in a celebration or a sentence?
10. The app should have a feature where instructors can anonymously admit "I want out" — because I can't say it out loud. Would anyone use it?

### 15 Years — The Time That Was Stolen
11. 15 years as an instructor — I started at 25, I'm 40 now. Where did my career go?
12. I planned to instruct for 2 years, build hours, then move on. That was 13 years ago. When did "temporary" become "permanent"?
13. Everyone says "time flies" — 15 years as a CFI didn't fly, it crawled. Why did the years feel so long when the days felt so short?
14. I've spent 15 years in the right seat of a Cessna 172 — 15 years. The same aircraft. The same patterns. The same lessons. Is that a career or a loop?
15. The app should show "average years as CFI before transition" — am I the outlier, or is 15 years the hidden norm?
16. I've watched three generations of students pass through my cockpit — my first students are now captains. I'm still in the right seat of a 172. What does that mean?
17. 15 years of "just one more year and I'll move on" — how many "one more years" before I admit I'm stuck?
18. I've been at the same flight school for 15 years — the walls know me, the aircraft know me, the pattern knows me. Is that comfort or captivity?
19. My 15-year plaque is on the wall — it feels like a tombstone. Does anyone else feel that about service awards?
20. The app mentions "Career Progression" — what progression? I've been at the same rank for 15 years. My career didn't progress, it calcified.

### 4,000 Hours — The Wrong Currency
21. 4,000 hours sounds impressive — until you realize 3,800 are in a Cessna 172. Does anyone in corporate aviation care about 172 time?
22. I have 4,000 hours and corporate operators want "turbine time" or "jet time." I have neither. What are 4,000 hours of piston time worth? Nothing?
23. The app's logbook tracks total time — but it should also track "employable time." My 4,000 hours are almost entirely unemployable in the corporate world.
24. I kept building hours thinking "4,000 will be the magic number" — it wasn't. 4,000 hours of the wrong type is worth less than 500 hours of the right type.
25. The app should have a "hours quality analyzer" — what % of your hours are employable for your target career? Mine would show 5%.
26. I have 4,000 hours and 200 multi — that ratio is embarrassing. 15 years and I barely built multi time. Was I asleep?
27. My students who went to regionals have 1,500 hours of turbine time now — I have 4,000 hours of pattern work. Who's the "experienced" pilot?
28. The app should warn CFIs: "instructing builds total time but NOT the type of time corporate operators want." Why doesn't anyone say that?
29. 4,000 hours of dual given — that's 4,000 hours of watching other people fly. I'm not even the one flying most of the time. Am I a pilot or a passenger?
30. The app's PRP should show "hours breakdown by type and role" — because "4,000 hours" on a CV hides the truth that it's all instruction time.

### The IFR Simulator Trap — Qualified But Caged
31. I'm an IFR simulator instructor — I spend 8 hours a day in a fake cockpit teaching people to fly on gauges. I'm qualified, not flying.
32. The sim is where they put senior instructors — it's considered a "promotion." I got promoted into a windowless room. Is that a reward or exile?
33. I'm type-rated on the sim's avionics suite — but it's not a real aircraft type rating. It's a sim qualification. Worthless outside the school.
34. The app mentions "W1000 avionics suite" in the Foundation Program — am I the kind of instructor who'd teach that? Is that my future?
35. Sim instructor hours don't count toward most flying jobs — I'm accumulating hours that don't count. Why did I accept this role?
36. I can fly a perfect ILS in the sim with my eyes closed — but I haven't flown in actual IMC in 3 years. Am I still a real instrument pilot?
37. The app should distinguish "simulator instruction hours" from "flight hours" in the logbook — because they're not the same thing, and employers know it.
38. My students pass their IR checkrides because of my sim training — and then they go fly in real weather while I stay in the box. Is that ironic or tragic?
39. I keep my IR current in the sim — but sim currency isn't the same as real-world currency. If I went to a corporate interview, would they see through that?
40. The app should have a "real vs sim hours" breakdown — because my 4,000 hours look very different when you remove the sim time.

### The Private Jet Dream — The Secret Inside
41. I want to fly private jets — Gulfstreams, Globals, Citations. That's been my dream for 10 years. I've never said it out loud to anyone in aviation.
42. Corporate flying is what I've wanted since I understood what it was — owners, VIPs, different destinations every day, no airline route structure. Why can't I get there?
43. I look up every time a Gulfstream flies overhead — and I'm standing on the ramp next to a 172. Is that hope or torture?
44. The app lists "Corporate Pilot Pathway" — but it's written for people STARTING in corporate. Where's the pathway for a 15-year CFI trying to BREAK IN?
45. Corporate operators want jet time, turbine time, VIP experience — I have 4,000 hours of teaching stalls to 18-year-olds. How do I bridge that gap?
46. I've applied to 30 corporate flight departments — zero responses. Is it my hours, my resume, or the fact that "15-year CFI" screams "couldn't get a real flying job"?
47. The app should have a "CFI to corporate" transition guide — because the gap is massive and no one addresses it.
48. I'd take a pay cut to fly right seat in a Citation — I just want IN. Is that desperation or clarity?
49. The app's "Get Hired" section lists corporate operators — does it show which ones hire from the CFI world, or only from 135/121 backgrounds?
50. I've considered buying a Citation Mustang type rating — but I learned my lesson watching Daniel Doyle buy an ATR rating. Should the app have stopped me?

### The Hypocrisy — Teaching Dreams I Haven't Achieved
51. I teach students about "career planning" and "setting goals" — I haven't achieved my own goal in 15 years. Am I qualified to teach career planning?
52. I tell students "visualize where you want to be in 5 years" — in 5 years I'll still be in this right seat. Should I stop teaching that lesson?
53. I teach the "Corporate Pilot Pathway" module in ground school — I've never flown corporate. Am I a fraud?
54. My students ask "why did you become a CFI instead of going to the airlines?" — I say "I love teaching." The real answer is "I couldn't get hired." When did the lie start?
55. I teach students about "networking" and "building connections" — I have no network outside my flight school. Am I teaching something I can't do?
56. The app mentions "mentorship knowledge" as an exam category — I'd pass that exam. But I'm failing at my own career. Is that irony?
57. I teach "persistence pays off" — I've been persistent for 15 years and it hasn't paid off. Should I stop teaching that?
58. A student asked me "what's your dream aircraft?" — I said "the 172, I love this plane." I lied. My dream aircraft is a Gulfstream G650. Why couldn't I say that?
59. I teach "don't let setbacks define you" — my entire career is a setback I've disguised as a vocation. Is that wisdom or self-deception?
60. The app should have a "mentor authenticity check" — are you teaching what you believe, or teaching what you're supposed to say?

### The Sim Pilot Identity — Not a Real Pilot Anymore?
61. I'm called a "sim pilot" behind my back by younger instructors — they don't say it to my face, but I hear it. Is that what I've become?
62. I haven't flown a real aircraft in 4 months — my job is 100% sim now. Am I still a pilot, or am I a computer operator?
63. The app should have a "pilot identity tracker" — when was your last actual flight? Mine was 127 days ago. Is that still being a pilot?
64. My medical is current, my licenses are current — but my hands haven't touched a real yoke in months. Currency on paper vs. currency in reality.
65. I could go fly a 172 on my day off — but I can't afford the rental rate on a CFI salary, and I'm tired of the aircraft anyway. Is that burnout or growth?
66. The sim is so realistic that I sometimes forget I'm not flying — then the session ends and I walk out into a windowless hallway. Is that a metaphor for my career?
67. My students respect my sim skills — but I see them looking at the charter pilots on the ramp with more admiration than they look at me. Am I imagining that?
68. The app mentions "simulator/FSTD time" in the logbook — should sim time even count? It's not real flying. Or is it?
69. I've become the instructor I swore I'd never be — the one who's been at the school so long they're part of the furniture. When did that happen?
70. If I left the sim and went back to flight instruction, I'd be bored. If I stay in the sim, I'm not flying. Is there a third option?

75. The app's "Get Funded" section is for students — where's the "fund your career transition" section for 15-year CFIs who want out?
76. I've saved $20K in 15 years — that's not a nest egg, that's an emergency fund. How do I fund a career pivot at 40 with $20K?
