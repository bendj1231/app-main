# Persona 20 — 2-Year Waiting Pilot (bedroom, weather app, email refresh)

## Profile
- **Hours**: 250
- **Licenses**: CPL/IR (current, barely)
- **Current role**: Unemployed / living with parents / part-time non-aviation job
- **Situation**: 2 years since graduation. No CFI job, no flying job. Obsessively checking email and weather.
- **App context**: Job search support, mental health, CFI hiring reality

## 100 Questions

### The Bedroom — Ground Zero of the Wait
1. I've been living in my childhood bedroom for 2 years since graduating with my CPL — I'm 24 and I sleep under my high school trophies. Is this what $80K of training gets you?
2. My daily routine: wake up, check email (no responses), check weather (for a flight I won't take), refresh airline job boards, eat, repeat. Is this a life?
3. I check my email 30+ times a day — every notification gives me a spike of hope, then a crash of disappointment. Is that an addiction or a coping mechanism?
4. My phone battery dies by 2 PM because I'm constantly refreshing job boards — LinkedIn, Indeed, airline career pages. Is that job searching or self-harm?
5. The app should have a "job search health" feature — track how often you check, and warn you when it becomes obsessive. Because I can't stop.
6. I haven't flown in 4 months — I can't afford aircraft rental. My logbook is frozen at 250 hours. Every day it gets harder to say I'm "current."
7. My flight school graduation photo is on my desk — 22-year-old me, smiling, holding my CPL. That person is gone. Who am I now?
8. My parents knock on my door to check on me — "any news?" I say "still looking." They nod. The disappointment is silent. Is that worse than yelling?
9. I've applied to 80 jobs in 2 years — 2 interviews, both rejections. I refresh my email hoping for #81 to be different. Is that hope or insanity?
10. The app should have a "waiting pilot support" section — because 2 years in a bedroom is not a career strategy, it's a mental health crisis.
