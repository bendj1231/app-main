# Persona 13 — Cadet Who Got In (250hrs, now FO, "dream" = food stamps)

- **Hours**: 300
- **Licenses**: CPL/IR, A320 or CRJ type rating

- **Situation**: The "success story" — on food stamps, living in crash pad, exhausted
- **App context**: First-year FO survival, pay reality, fatigue management
