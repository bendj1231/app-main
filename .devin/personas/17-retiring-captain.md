# Persona 17 — Retiring Captain (64, 11 months left, no pension, no plan)

- **Hours**: 28,000
- **Licenses**: ATPL, multiple type ratings
- **Current role**: Captain at major airline, 40-year career
- **Situation**: 11 months to mandatory retirement at 65. No pension, $180K saved, identity crisis.
- **App context**: Retirement preparation, legacy, post-retirement career, spouse transition
