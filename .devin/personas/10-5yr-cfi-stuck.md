# Persona 10 — 5-Year CFI, Type Rated, Stuck (2000-5000 hours)

## Profile
- **Hours**: 2,000-5,000
- **Licenses**: CPL, IR, CFI/CFII/MEI, type rated (e.g. ATR)
- **Situation**: Exceeds every posted minimum. Still instructing after 5 years. No airline call.
- **App context**: The app's pathways don't address the "stuck timer" problem

## 100 Questions

1. Airlines say "1,500 hours minimum" — I have 3,200. Why am I still instructing?
2. The app lists airline minimums at 1,200-4,000 hours — I exceed the minimum at every regional. So what's the REAL minimum?

4. I watch 1,500-hour Riddle grads get hired while I sit at 3,200 — is it the degree, the connections, or something I don't see?
5. The "pilot shortage" is screamed from every rooftop — I'm a 3,200-hour type-rated CFI and I can't get an interview. Define "shortage" for me.
6. Is the 1,500-hour number a public minimum and a private maximum? "Don't call us unless you're between 1,500 and 2,000"?
7. I have more hours than half the regional captains flying right now — but I'm still in the right seat of a 172. What am I missing?
8. The app should show "real hire range" not "posted minimum" — because the minimum is clearly not the minimum. How do I build that data?
9. I keep hearing "we're hiring!" from airlines — but my 3,200-hour application gets auto-rejected. Is the hiring real or PR?
10. At what hour count do I become "overqualified" and toxic to airlines? Is 3,200 already past it?
