## 100 Questions

3. I fly a CRJ-200 — 50 seats, no first class, no WiFi, broken APU half the time. This is the aircraft the airline puts new hires on. Is this the dream?
4. I've been at the regional for 11 months — I've called in sick 3 times (actually sick), and scheduling called each time to ask "are you SURE?"
5. My first paycheck was $1,847 — for 2 weeks. After student loan payment, rent (crash pad), and food, I had $180 left. For a month. I'm an airline pilot.
6. The app should show "first-year total compensation" — not just hourly rate, because the hourly rate is a lie when you only fly 75 hours.
