# Persona 09 — Daniel Doyle (700hrs, ATR typed, CFI, AMT, gave up aviation)

## Profile
- **Hours**: 700
- **Licenses**: CPL, IR, CFI, ATR type rating, AMT license, B737 AMT rating
- **Degree**: 4-year aviation degree (didn't need it)
- **Total invested**: $200K+
- **Situation**: Every trap hit. Every wall hit. Walking away from aviation entirely.
- **App context**: The app is built from Daniel's scars. Every feature is a mistake he made.

## 100 Questions

2. I could've had my CPL at 20 with no debt — instead I graduated at 24 with a degree I didn't need and $120K in student loans. How does the industry sleep at night?
3. The university aviation program sold me "you'll be airline-ready" — I was airline-ready at the license stage, the degree just made me poorer. Is that fraud?
4. My college advisor said "get the degree first, airlines require it" — was that true in 2018, or was it always a lie?
5. I watched kids who did fast-track PPL at 17 get to 1,500 hours before I even graduated — what was the point of my degree?
6. The app I'm building should have a warning: "Do NOT get a degree unless you've confirmed your target airline requires it." Why doesn't any school say that?
7. I want to put a "degree ROI calculator" in the app — 4 years of lost wages + tuition vs. fast-track + CFI hours. Would that have stopped me?
8. My degree is in aviation — it's worthless outside aviation. So now that I'm leaving, I have a $80K piece of paper that qualifies me for nothing.
9. Should the app have a "before you enroll in college" intervention page that says "have you considered Part 61?"?
10. I was 18 and trusted the system — the system used me. How do I make the app the thing that stops the next Daniel Doyle?
