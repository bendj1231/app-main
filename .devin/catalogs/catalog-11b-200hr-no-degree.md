**Highest-priority no-degree builds (ranked):**

1. **No-Degree Airline Directory + Degree Enforcement Database** (Free) — the app has airline expectations data but NO degree-requirement column. This is the #1 thing this user needs: which airlines will actually look at their application. Q3, Q9, Q21, Q81, Q84.

2. **R-ATP Complete Guide for No-Degree Pilots** ($25) — R-ATP is completely absent from the app. It's the one regulatory path that could save this user 300-500 hours, and the app doesn't mention it at all. Q31–Q40.

3. **No-Degree Aviation Career Directory** (Free) — the app has 10 career categories but doesn't flag which are degree-agnostic. Bush, ag, firefighting, humanitarian, cargo feeder, charter, survey, banner, skydiving — none require degrees. This needs to be explicit. Q61–Q70.

4. **Degree + Flight Training Integrated Program** ($499) — the app's biggest structural gap. It has flight training pathways but zero degree planning. For a 22-year-old deciding whether to go back to school, this program could be life-changing. Q51–Q60.

5. **"Should I Quit Aviation?" Honest Assessment Session** ($69) — the most sensitive consultation in the entire catalog. This user is at maximum despair. The app's PSA content validates their pain but offers no human support. Q98.

6. **No-Degree Success Stories Collection** ($12) — the PSA is full of failure stories ("Golden CFI who can't escape," "200-hour graduate with no placement"). It needs success stories of pilots who made it without degrees. Q92, Q93.

7. **Airline Expectations Degree Filter** — adding a "degree: required/preferred/not required" column to the existing PortalAirlineExpectationsPage is a simple data enhancement with massive impact. Q81, Q84.

8. **"Valley of Death" Survival Guide for No-Degree Pilots** ($15) — the PSA content documents the despair. This guide provides actionable survival strategies: budgeting, side jobs, mental health, family communication. Q91, Q95, Q96.

This user segment — 200 hours, no degree, $80K debt — is the most underserved in the entire app. The PSA content speaks *about* them but not *to* them. The career pathways assume qualifications they don't have. The Foundation Program helps with mentorship but doesn't address the degree wall. The Transition Program targets airline interviews they can't get yet. The No-Degree Airline Directory and R-ATP Guide are the two products that could make the difference between this pilot quitting aviation and making it through.
