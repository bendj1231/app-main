**The answer to Q100 — "If the app can't help me, who CAN it help?"**

If the app can't help Crisostomo — a 12,000-hour captain who's bored, trapped, and willing to invest — then it can only help students and low-timers. But here's the thing: Crisostomo's problem IS the universal pilot problem at the senior level. Recognition isn't portable. Experience isn't valued outside one airline. Transition pathways don't exist. Seniority is a prison.

If the app solves Crisostomo's problem, it solves the problem for every senior pilot in the world. And when senior pilots use the platform, two things happen: (1) they become mentors for the juniors (the Foundation Program's mentor chain), and (2) they become the platform's most credible advocates — 12,000-hour captains saying "this platform gave me a way out" is the most powerful marketing the app could ever have.

Crisostomo isn't just a user. He's the proof that the platform works at the highest level. If it works for him, it works for everyone. And his investment — emotional and financial — is the validation that the platform's mission matters to the people the industry ignores most.

The app already has the PSA content documenting Crisostomo's trap ("12 years erased"). It already has the seniority risk calculator. It already has the CCQ data. It already has the "portable recognition" concept. What it doesn't have is the **pathway out** — the mid-career transition pathway, the sim instructor job board, the lifestyle comparison tool, and the career achievement tracker that turns 12 years from a prison sentence into a portfolio.

Build those, and the app doesn't just document Crisostomo's trap. It hands him the key.
