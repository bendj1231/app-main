**The answer to Q100 — "Calling or mirage?"**

The app can tell the difference — with data. A calling survives the ROI calculator, the family impact assessment, the age trajectory tool, and the reality check. A mirage doesn't. If you run the numbers and the numbers say "don't do it" and you still want to — that might be a calling. If you run the numbers and the numbers say "don't do it" and you feel relief — that was a mirage, and you just saved your family £275K.

The app's job isn't to tell you "yes" or "no." The app's job is to give you the data, the tools, and the mentor sessions to make the most informed decision of your career. Whether that decision is "go" or "stay" — the app has done its job if the decision is informed.

The AOM-to-pilot pathway is the most underserved career switch in aviation. The app has the AOMTrap.tsx component documenting the reverse (students pushed into AOM), but nothing for AOMs who want to go the other way. The Career Switcher ROI Calculator and the Family Impact Assessment are the two tools that could prevent the next "I love flying but I miss the stability" story — or confirm that the switch is right, with eyes wide open.
