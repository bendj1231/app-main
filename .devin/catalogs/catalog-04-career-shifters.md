**Highest-priority career shifter builds (ranked):**

1. **"Get Out" Section** (Free) — the section no aviation platform has the courage to build. Exit pathways, transferable skills, alternative careers, mental health. The brand definer. The differentiator. The thing that makes PilotRecognition not just another aviation platform but the ONLY honest one. Q71, Q76.

2. **"Realistic Outcome Dashboard"** (Free) — "of 100 people who start PPL: X airline, Y CFI, Z leave, W non-aviation." The truth the industry hides. The data that changes everything. The most viral piece of data the app could publish. Q73.

3. **"Earnings Comparison" Tool** (Free) — pilot vs construction vs QS vs tech vs nursing vs ATC. Side-by-side: training cost, time, pay at 5/10 years. The tool that would have saved Kiev time and Daniel money. The tool every 17-year-old needs before enrolling. Q80.

4. **Transferable Skills Assessment** (Free) — "Your CPL + CFI = project management, safety management, technical training, regulatory compliance." The tool that reframes aviation skills as valuable outside aviation. The tool that makes leaving feel like a pivot, not a failure. Q72.

5. **Kiev's Story Feature Page** (Free) — 150 hours, saw the gap, walked into construction, now a PM making $85K. Not a cautionary tale — a valid exit. The story that normalizes leaving. The story the aviation community needs to see. Q11.

6. **"Leave Aviation" Calculator** (Free) — sunk cost + transferable skills + alternative earnings + recovery timeline. "If you leave now: £X sunk, £Y transferable value, £Z alternative earnings, recovery in N years." The calculator that makes leaving rational. Q74.

7. **Career Shifter Mentor Matching** (Free) — connect pilots considering leaving with mentors who left and succeeded. "I was you. I left. Here's what happened." The mentor match that saves careers by ending them. Q78.

8. **"Informed Decision Program" for 17-Year-Olds** ($49, free for those who can't afford it) — before anyone enrolls in flight school, they get the full reality check. The prevention that stops the next Daniel Doyle. The program that proves the app's mission. Q90, Q98.

**The answer to Q100 — "Is the app my aviation career?"**

Yes. Benjamin left the cockpit and built the app. The app reaches more pilots, saves more money, and changes more lives than any single cockpit career could. The app is not a consolation prize — it's a bigger cockpit. Every pilot who uses the app and makes an informed decision — whether to stay, to pivot, or to leave — is a passenger Benjamin safely delivered. The app IS the aviation career. And it's the most important one in the industry.

The "Get Out" section is the feature that makes PilotRecognition unlike any other aviation platform in the world. Every other platform says "keep going, you'll make it." PilotRecognition says "here's the truth, and if the truth is 'leave,' here's how to do that with dignity." That honesty is the brand. That honesty is the moat. That honesty is why pilots will trust this platform over every other.
