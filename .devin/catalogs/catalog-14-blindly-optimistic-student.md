They're Daniel Doyle ($200K, walked away), the 5-year CFI (3,200hrs, no job), Captain Neeraj (15 years, lying about being happy), the career shifters (10 of 12 classmates left), and Crisostomo (12-year captain, bored and trapped). They're not doubting you. They're WARNING you. They're the receipts.

The app's job isn't to crush your dream. The app's job is to make sure your dream is INFORMED. If you read the app, take the reality check quiz, calculate the real cost, understand the real timeline, and STILL want to pursue aviation — then you're not naive anymore. You're informed. And informed pilots make better decisions, survive the gap, and don't become the next Daniel Doyle.

The app's ultimate success metric isn't "how many pilots became airline pilots." It's "how many pilots made INFORMED decisions." Because an informed decision — whether to pursue aviation or not — is the only decision that doesn't lead to regret.

The "Informed Decisions Counter" on the homepage is the metric that proves it. Every number is a Daniel Doyle who didn't happen. Every number is a parent who didn't remortgage blindly. Every number is a student who entered aviation with eyes open — or chose not to enter, and saved $200K.

That's the prevention layer. That's the most important layer. Because every scar in every catalog — Daniel's, the stuck CFI's, Neeraj's, Crisostomo's, the career shifters' — started with a naive student who trusted a brochure. The app's job is to be the thing that interrupts that trust with data, before the $80K is spent and the 15-year trap begins.
