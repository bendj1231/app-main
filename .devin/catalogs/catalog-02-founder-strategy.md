**The answer to Q100 — "monument or business?" — is this:**

It's a business IF you can prove that a 200hr pilot who uses PilotRecognition gets to 1,500hrs and an airline job faster than one who doesn't. Build the metrics to track that. If the data shows it, you have a business worth funding. If the data shows it doesn't, you have a monument — and you'll know which one before you spend years building the wrong thing.

The scars are good product instincts. The validation is whether those instincts help other people, not just you. Build the success metrics first. Everything else follows from that answer.
