**Highest-priority ATPL builds (ranked):**

1. **ATPL Question Bank (Adaptive)** ($29/mo or $199/yr) — the exam portal exists but has zero ATPL-specific content. 14 exams × 10,000+ questions is the #1 gap. Highest recurring revenue potential. Q38, Q14, Q88.

2. **14 ATPL Subject Master Bundle** ($299) — Met, Air Law, Navigation, Performance are the #1 failure subjects. The app has zero ATPL subject content. Q21–Q35.

3. **ATPL Progress Tracker Pro** — auto-tracks 14 exams, expiry dates, hours toward 1,500, frozen/unfrozen status. PathwayProgressTracker has an ATPL field but doesn't track the 14 exams or expiry. Q2, Q6, Q10, Q39, Q85, Q94, Q95.

4. **MCC/JOC Complete Program** ($399) — the Transition Program covers CRM/multi-crew conceptually via EBT/CBTA, but there's no structured MCC/JOC prep product. European airlines require these. Q46–Q65.

5. **Type Rating Prep Program** ($299, A320 or B737) — the app has type rating *search* and aircraft *specs* but no TR *prep* product. Self-funded TR is a $30K+ decision (BusinessPlanPage explicitly calls out the "$30K ATR type rating" trap). Q66–Q75.

6. **Airline Interview Mock** ($149) — the enterprise InterviewAssessmentForm exists with EBT/CBTA 9-competency scoring. Adapting it for pilot-facing mock interviews is a natural extension. Q100.

7. **Airline Aptitude Test Practice Platform** ($19/mo) — airlines use aptitude tests (numerical, verbal, spatial, multitasking) that pilots can't currently practice anywhere in the app. Q98.

8. **Type Rating Relief Grant** (charitable) — directly addresses the BusinessPlanPage's "$30K ATR type rating" investment trap story. Strongest charitable brand alignment. Q66, Q74.

The app's existing Emirates ATPL Pathway page, Transition Program (CRM/multi-crew/EBT-CBTA), enterprise interview assessment infra, type rating search, and airline expectations data make ATPL the stage with the **most existing infrastructure to build on** — but also the **largest content gap** (zero ATPL theory content, zero MCC/JOC structured prep, zero aptitude test practice). The ATPL Question Bank alone could be a six-figure recurring revenue product.
