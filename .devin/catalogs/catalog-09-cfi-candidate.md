**Highest-priority CFI builds (ranked):**

1. **FOI Master Course + Flashcard App** ($49 + $9/mo) — FOI is the #1 CFI pain point. Dry, dense, mandatory. The mentor modules cover teaching philosophy but not the FAA FOI curriculum. Q21–Q30.

2. **CFI Lesson Plan Library + Builder** ($49 + $15/mo) — every CFI candidate needs lesson plans. The app has zero lesson plan infrastructure. This is the #1 practical gap. Q31–Q40.

3. **CFI Written Exam Engine (FOI + CFI Knowledge)** ($15/mo) — the exam portal exists with 4 categories but none map to FAA FOI/CFI written exams. High-recurring revenue. Q17–Q20, Q30, Q55.

4. **CFI Oral Mock Exam** ($149) — high-margin consultation, leverages existing mentor network. The CFI oral is notoriously the hardest in aviation. Q51–Q58.

5. **Student Progress Tracking + Digital Endorsement System** — the mentor supervision/analytics/logbook infra exists; adapting it for CFI→student tracking + digital signoff is a natural extension. Q63, Q66, Q67, Q80, Q84.

6. **"Golden CFI" Escape Grant** (charitable) — directly addresses the app's own PSA content about the "Golden CFI Who Can't Escape." Strongest brand alignment of any proposed solution. Q97, Q100.

7. **CFI Complete Program** ($499) — formalizes the MindMap's existing "Instructor Ready" claim into a structured, priced product. The Wing Mentorship Program already says it prepares pilots for "CFI practicals" — this product delivers on that promise.

The app's existing architecture (mentor modules, mentor recognition system, mentor logbook, mentor matching, exam portal, Foundation/Transition programs) is *uniquely positioned* to support CFI products — more so than any other pilot stage. The mentor modules literally teach teaching methodology, and the MindMap explicitly claims "Instructor Ready" as an outcome. A CFI Complete Program is the most natural revenue extension of the platform's existing identity.
