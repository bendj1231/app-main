| Tier | Audience | Price | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | All pilots | $0 | Logbook, pathways, exam portal basics, scholarship directory |
| **Recognition+** | Serious students | $12/mo or $99/yr (regional) | Unlimited pathways, priority matching, AI strategist |
| **Programs** | Career-stage specific | $49–$399 one-time | Foundation, Transition, Cadet Bootcamp, MCC/JOC |
| **Consultation** | 1:1 seekers | $75–$150/session | Career strategy, logbook audit, mock interview |
| **Digital Products** | Self-study | $19–$89 | CV guide, interview bundle, logbook export, type rating kit |
| **Enterprise** | Operators/airlines | $1,000/yr flat | Pull API, Recognition Score, candidate pool |
| **Verification** | Operators (bulk) | $25–$200/check | Background, credential, flight-hours verification |
| **Referral/Investment** | Partners | Commission/lead fee | Loans, insurance, AME, type rating providers |
| **Charitable** | Underserved | $0 (donor-funded) | Scholarships, veteran bridge, pay-it-forward mentorship |

**Recommendation on what to build first:** The highest-margin, lowest-effort wins are the **Consultation** tier (you already have the mentor network and EBT video scoring infra — just add a booking + payment flow) and the **Type Rating Prep Kit** digital product (you already have rich aircraft/manufacturer data in `data/aircraft-manufacturers.ts` and `data/aircraft-models.ts`). The **Income Share Agreement** is the highest-leverage long-term play but requires legal/financial structuring.
