**The answer to Q100 — "Did I win, even though I lost?"**

Yes. Daniel lost $200K, 8 years, two type ratings, a degree, an AMT license, and his place in aviation. But if the app prevents even ONE person from self-funding an ATR type rating without a conditional offer, Daniel won. If it prevents ONE person from getting a degree they didn't need, Daniel won. If it tells ONE person "stop, you're in a sunk cost trap" before they spend another $25K, Daniel won.

The "Prevented Decisions" tracker is the metric that proves it. Build it. Put it on the homepage. Let it count up in real time. Every number is a Daniel Doyle who didn't happen.

Daniel didn't lose. Daniel became the reason the app exists. And the app is the reason the next Daniel won't have to make the same call.
