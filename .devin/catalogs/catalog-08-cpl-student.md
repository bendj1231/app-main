**Highest-priority CPL builds:**

1. **CPL Written Exam Question Bank + Adaptive Engine** ($15/mo or Recognition+) — the exam portal exists but is mentorship-focused, not CPL-written-specific. This is the #1 gap. Q92, Q93.
2. **Commercial Maneuvers Complete Bundle** ($89) — chandelles, lazy eights, eights-on-pylons, power-off 180 are the #1 CPL pain point. Q31–Q40.
3. **Maneuver Video Evaluation Service** ($79/maneuver) — leverages existing EBT video scoring infrastructure. High-margin, differentiates from competitors. Q31–Q40, Q95.
4. **CPL Progress Tracker Pro** — auto-validates logbook against CPL minimums by authority. The logbook exists; this is an enhancement layer. Q4, Q8, Q13.
5. **Low-Timer → First Job Program** ($299) — the app's Pilot Gap Module and shortage content address this conceptually, but there's no structured program for landing the *first paid flying job* (as opposed to the Transition Program which targets airlines). Q100.

Want me to start implementing? I'd suggest beginning with the **CPL Written Exam Prep page** (extending the existing examination portal) or the **Commercial Maneuvers Bundle** product in the store. I can also persist all three catalogs (career, PPL, CPL) to a strategy doc for reference.
