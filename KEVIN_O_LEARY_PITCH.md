## The Problem: A $50,000 Trap

**Floor 0:** Pilot graduates with 200 hours. Promised airline jobs. Still waiting. The 2015 batch is **still** in line for instructor positions. $50,000 sits unused.

**Floor 1:** Flight instructors with 5,000-6,000 hours. 15 years experience. Stuck because nobody's leaving Floor 2. They try to jump ahead — get sent back. Wasted time.

**Floor 2:** The collapse point. Everyone fighting for recognition. No communication. Pilots fly blind on requirements.

**Floor 3:** Airline pilots with 12+ years. Bored. Want change. Trapped by seniority. Captain goes back to First Officer. Pay cut. Position sacrifice.

**The industry is clogged. Pilots are burning $50K and years of their lives.**

## The Solution: Recognition as Currency

**Not a job board. A recognition framework.**

- **Pathway Cards** — not job listings. Show requirements and what you're missing.
- **Pulling System** — pilots submit interest, airlines pull from live database.
- **Live Real-Time Profiles** — not static CVs. Update as pilots log hours.
- **Recognition Score** — portable currency. Capabilities travel, not airline seniority.
- **EBT Video Scoring** — proprietary IP. Behavioral scores airlines actually want.

### 2. Recognition Profile
- **Free:** Basic matching, 2 gaps shown, 3 pathways/month
- **Plus ($99/year):** Full comparison, unlimited pathways, priority matching

### 3. Pathways
- **Free:** Public pathways (airlines, cargo, cadet programs)
- **Plus ($99/year):** Unlimited views, full insights
- **Premium ($49 each or $199/year):** Private sector pathways, advanced analytics, priority matching

### 4. Airlines/Operators (B2B)
- **Free:** Post pathways, view profiles
- **Enterprise ($1,000/month):** Pull API, unlimited pulls, EBT video access
- **Success fee:** $500 per hire
