# Pilot Shortage Association (PSA) Universal Commercial Framework

## 26-Pillar Advocacy Grid for pilotshortage.org

**Date:** May 30, 2026  
**Classification:** Non-Profit Advocacy Architecture — Transparency Framework  
**Mission:** Unclog the Pipeline Through Systemic Change

---

## Overview

**PSA treats all 26 stakeholders as nodes in a transparency network.**

Unlike pilotrecognition.com's commercial verification framework, pilotshortage.org operates as a **non-profit advocacy collective**. We don't sell verification — we **demand accountability**.

Our 26 pillars span:

- **Voice** (pilots speaking)
- **Verification** (proof of credentials)
- **Pathways** (transparent requirements)

## Cluster A: Core Advocacy Participants (The Voice)

**Nodes speaking truth to power, establishing the clogged pipeline narrative.**

### Pillar 1: The Anonymous Pilot Storyteller

- **Role:** Primary voice of the clogged pipeline
- **Actions:** Shares training investment, placement struggles, survival story
- **Protection:** Identity anonymized for whistleblower safety
- **Output:** Anonymous testimony powering industry pressure

### Pillar 2: The Verified Pilot Contributor

- **Role:** Credibility-backed storyteller
- **Actions:** Verifies credentials via pilotrecognition.com, then speaks
- **Benefit:** Unimpeachable testimony — credentials proven, story real

### Pillar 3: The Flight Instructor Advocate

- **Role:** 5,000-6,000 hour expert with no voice
- **Actions:** Speaks about dignity gap, recognition deficit
- **Goal:** Industry acknowledgment of instructor expertise

### Pillar 4: The Airline Captain (Trapped)

- **Role:** 12-year senior pilot handcuffed to position
- **Actions:** Explains seniority trap, why leaving = First Officer reset
- **Pain:** Capabilities real, but recognition non-portable

### Pillar 5: The 200-Hour Graduate

- **Role:** Fresh CPL holder with $50K+ investment
- **Actions:** Documents "scan the code" rejection, no placement pathway
- **Reality:** Qualified by Airbus standards, rejected by industry practice

## Cluster B: Verification Partners (The Proof)

**Nodes providing credential verification — not for profit, for credibility.**

### Pillar 6: pilotrecognition.com (Primary Verification Partner)

- **Role:** Non-commercial verification backbone
- **Functions:** Logbook verification, flight hours validation, license confirmation
- **Integration:** Free for PSA contributors
- **Output:** Verified pilot = credible voice

### Pillar 7: CAAP (Philippines Authority)

- **Role:** Sovereign registry partner
- **Validates:** CPL, PEL, type ratings for Filipino pilots
- **Use:** Foundation verification for Southeast Asian contributors

### Pillar 8: FAA PRD (US Authority)

- **Role:** US registry for American pilots
- **Validates:** Airman certificates, check-ride history
- **Use:** Verification for North American contributors

### Pillar 9: EASA & European CAAs

- **Role:** European licensing authorities
- **Validates:** EU pilot credentials
- **Use:** Verification for European contributors

### Pillar 10: National Medical Examiners

- **Role:** Class 1/2 medical validators
- **Validates:** Medical certificate status
- **Use:** Health verification layer

---

## Cluster C: Pathway Transparency Partners (The Bridge)

**Nodes showing pilots exactly what's needed — before they apply.**

### Pillar 11: pilotcareerpathways.com

- **Role:** Transparent pathway provider
- **Functions:** Airline-specific requirements, gap analysis, career trajectory
- **Integration:** Embedded in PSA for contributor benefit
- **Value:** No more "flying blind"

### Pillar 12: ATO Partnerships

- **Role:** Flight school alignment
- **Function:** Bridge training to employment reality
- **Goal:** Schools teach to actual airline requirements, not just 200 hours

### Pillar 13: Airline Collaboration (Etihad, Cebu Pacific, etc.)

- **Role:** Industry partners willing to be transparent
- **Function:** Publish real requirements, not "1500 hours + scan the code"
- **Benefit:** Airlines get verified, prepared applicants

### Pillar 14: Manufacturer Partnerships (Airbus, Boeing)

- **Role:** OEM acknowledgment of 200-hour qualification
- **Function:** OEM states: "Our SOPs work with 200-hour pilots"
- **Leverage:** Use OEM standards to pressure airlines
