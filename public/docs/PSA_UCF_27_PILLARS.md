### Pillar 27: Sovereign Data Custody Architecture

- **Role:** Privacy-first data governance with contractual liability shields
- **Function:** Three-tier architecture ensuring pilots control their credentials
- **Architecture:**
  - **Tier 1:** Pilot Wallet (device) holds actual W3C Verifiable Credentials
  - **Tier 2:** pilotrecognition.com stores only text claims (no documents)
  - **Tier 3:** Regional authorities (CAAP, FAA, Veremark) manage physical documents
- **Privacy:** Physical documents stay in-country; platform receives only "verified" status
- **Compliance:** Minimizes GDPR special category exposure (health data)
- **Benefit:** If platform breached, attackers get only text — not medical certs or passports
- **Pilot Control:** Cryptographic credentials held locally; pilots can delete anytime

#### Contractual Safeguards (All Verification Provider MSAs)

**Purpose Limitation Clause:**
- Providers process medical certificate data SOLELY for verification status determination
- PROHIBITED: Secondary use, third-party sharing, retention beyond 90 days
- Providers NEVER transmit medical images/PDFs to pilotrecognition.com
- Only classification metadata (Class 1/2/3, expiry date, status) crosses the API

**Retention and Deletion:**
- Maximum 90-day retention for medical certificate images
- Automatic deletion post-verification
- Deletion certificates provided upon request
- 5-day deletion certification upon pilot account closure

**Audit Rights:**
- Annual third-party security assessments
- Data retention log reviews
- Technical verification that no medical images enter PilotRecognition systems

**Liability Allocation:**
- **Provider bears FULL LIABILITY** for medical data breaches, GDPR Art 9 violations
- **PilotRecognition liability LIMITED TO:** text claims (medical class, expiry) only
- Provider indemnifies PilotRecognition for regulatory fines arising from Provider's processing

#### Technical Proof Points (Defensible Architecture)

**Evidence That Medical Data Never Touches Our Infrastructure:**

| Control Layer | Implementation | Verification Method |
|-------------|----------------|-------------------|
| API Schema | Webhook accepts ONLY: `medical_class`, `expiry_date`, `status` | Payload validation rejects `medical_certificate_image`, `doctor_notes`, `examination_results` |
| Database RLS | No storage buckets for medical documents | Quarterly audit shows ZERO medical image storage |
| Network Layer | Firewall rules block medical file upload signatures | Access logs demonstrate rejection of document uploads |
| Verification Flow | Veremark API → Status JSON → Text Database | Architecture diagrams show no document transmission path |

**The Defensible Position:**
> *"Our systems technically cannot receive medical data. Our API rejects it. Our RLS blocks it. Our architecture prevents it. We process only classification metadata — never health data. This is auditable, verifiable, and contractually enforced."*

#### Legal Basis: Operational Necessity Defense

**GDPR Art 6.1(f) — Legitimate Interest Assessment (Employment Eligibility)**

| Element | Assessment | Evidence |
|---------|------------|----------|
| **Legitimate Interest** | Aviation safety + airline operational integrity | EASA Part-MED, FAA Part 67, CAAP CAR Part 2, ICAO Annex 1 |
| **Necessity** | Cannot assess pathway eligibility without medical currency | Airlines legally cannot employ pilots without medical verification |
| **Balancing Test** | Pilot privacy vs. public safety | Minimal data (class/expiry only), opt-in per pathway, delete anytime |

**Processing Purpose:**
> Medical status data (`medical_class`, `expiry_date`, `verification_status`) is processed SOLELY to:
> 1. Determine pilot eligibility for specific career pathways requiring medical currency
> 2. Enable airlines (data controllers) to comply with statutory medical fitness requirements
> 3. Facilitate safe employment matching between verified pilots and regulated operators

**Data Flow:**
```
Regional Verifier (CAAP/Veremark/FAA)
    ↓ [Medical document processing — their legal basis]
Status JSON: {"medical_class": "Class 1", "expiry": "2026-05-02", "status": "valid"}
    ↓ [API schema validation — WE ONLY RECEIVE THIS]
pilotrecognition.com Tier 2 Database
    ↓ [Employment eligibility status — our legitimate interest]
Airline Data Controller
    ↓ [Contractual relationship with pilot]
Pilot Employment Decision
```

**The Key Legal Argument:**
> We do NOT process "data concerning health" (Art 9). We process **employment eligibility status** derived from health verification — analogous to:
> - Credit scoring: "approved/declined" (not full credit report)
> - Background checks: "cleared/not cleared" (not full criminal record)
> - Employment verification: "employed 2020-2024" (not full HR file)
>
> **GDPR Art 9.2(b) applies:** Processing necessary for "carrying out the obligations and exercising specific rights of the controller or of the data subject in the field of employment and social security and social protection law"

#### Purpose Limitation Declaration

**What We Process (Employment Eligibility Data Only):**
| Data Field | Source | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|------------|--------|---------|-------------|
| `medical_class` | Veremark/CAAP | Verify pathway eligibility | Art 6.1(f) Legitimate Interest |
| `medical_expiry` | Veremark/CAAP | Confirm currency for airline | Art 6.1(f) + Art 9.2(b) Employment |
| `verification_status` | Internal calc | Access control to premium pathways | Art 6.1(b) Contract performance |
| `pathway_eligibility` | Aggregated | Match pilots to appropriate opportunities | Art 6.1(f) Legitimate Interest |

**What We NEVER Process (Excluded by Architecture):**
- ❌ Medical diagnoses or conditions
- ❌ Medical examination details or results  
- ❌ Medical images, PDFs, or scanned documents
- ❌ Health history or physician notes
- ❌ Medical limitation specifics (beyond Class 1/2/3 classification)
- ❌ Treatment information or prescriptions

**The Legal Shield:**
> *"PilotRecognition's infrastructure is architecturally incapable of receiving medical documents. Our API schema explicitly rejects medical image fields. Our RLS policies block document storage. We receive ONLY classification metadata necessary for employment eligibility verification. Regional verification providers (CAAP, Veremark, FAA) process the underlying medical data under their own legal basis — we process the employment status derived from their verification."*
