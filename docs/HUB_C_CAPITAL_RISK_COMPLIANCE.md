## Pillar 10: Legal & Regulatory Bodies

### The Role

**Sovereign frameworks governing entire operation.**

**Entities:**
- National Privacy Commission (NPC) — Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012
- FAA (Federal Aviation Administration)
- Regional civil aviation authorities (CAAP, EASA, etc.)

### Macro Value: Primary Legal Shield

**The Protection:**
- Architecture is **completely stateless**
- Platform **doesn't store or alter data**

**When legal/regulatory audit occurs:**
- Immutable transaction logs demonstrate:
  1. Pilot gave **explicit consent**
  2. Regional provider **executed the check**
  3. Platform merely served as **neutral, accurate display interface**

**The Defense:**
> "We don't possess the data. We don't process the verification. We only display what the sovereign registry and regional proxy confirmed."

**Result:** Liability bypasses platform, lands on responsible node.
