# Pilot Shortage Association — Narrative & Persona Framework

## The Core Thesis

**There is no pilot shortage. There is a clogged pipeline.**

Thousands of pilots exist. They are trained, credentialed, and ready. But they are **stuck** — trapped in a system that takes their money, promises them careers, then leaves them stranded with no guidance, no transparency, and no pathway forward.

The industry claims "pilot shortage" to justify low wages, aggressive recruitment, and training bonds. The reality? **The shortage is the gap between 200-hour graduates and 1,500-hour airline requirements.** A gap that costs $500,000 to bridge. A gap created by the **2013 law** (post-crash rule change) that crashed pilot careers.

### The 2013 Law — The Breaking Point
- **The Regulation:** After a crash, the 1,500-hour minimum rule was implemented
- **The Intent:** Safety. The aviation industry has the safest record now.
- **The Unintended Consequence:** **Career genocide for pilots.** The gap between CPL (200 hours) and airline minimum (1,500 hours) became a $500,000 chasm.
- **The Result:** A drastic crash in pilot careers. Mass abandonment. "I can't find a position" became the anthem.
- **The Math:** 200 → 1,500 = 1,300 hours missing. At $400/hour = **$520,000 to qualify for an entry-level job.**

### The Real Shortage Definition
> **"It's not about how many pilots there are. It's not about the amount of pilots. It's about the industry's broken pipeline — the track to get from graduation to a job position."**

The shortage is **structural**, not numerical. The pilots exist. The pathway doesn't.

### Aviation Collapsing at Its Base
- **Pilots giving up:** Changing careers entirely. The smart money leaves aviation.
- **Instructor backlog:** Economic cycle means instructors leave once every 3-4 years. Current backlog: **10+ years.**
- **The 2015 vs 2025 competition:** Batch of 2015 still waiting. New 2025 graduates joining the same queue.
- **The math:** More graduates entering. Same instructor slots. **Queue extends indefinitely.**

**If pilots are giving up and changing careers, the shortage will increase.** Not because there aren't enough pilots, but because the **pipeline is so broken that trained pilots abandon the profession entirely.**

### Where Pilots Currently Testify
- **Reddit:** Anonymous venting, scattered stories
- **YouTube:** Complaints, warnings to future students
- **The Problem:** **No verified platform.** No credibility. No collective voice.

**pilotshortage.org is that place.** Verified pilots. Real names (or protected identity). Legally vetted stories. One platform where the truth can be told — and heard.
