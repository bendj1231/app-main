Today marks a significant milestone for PilotRecognition as we announce our strategic partnership with **Veremark**, a global leader in background screening and credential verification. This collaboration brings enterprise-grade verification capabilities directly to aviation professionals, starting with our launch market in the Philippines.

## Why Veremark?

Veremark operates in **180+ countries** with particular strength in Europe and Asia-Pacific through their Singapore hub. Their API-first approach aligns perfectly with our vision of real-time, portable pilot credentials that carriers can trust.

### Key Verification Capabilities

Through this partnership, PilotRecognition members can now verify:

- **Professional Qualifications** — CAAP licenses, type ratings, ELP certificates
- **Education Records** — ATO graduation, course completions, academic credentials  
- **Employment History** — Previous operators, hours logged, position tenure
- **Regulatory Compliance** — Medical certificates, NBI clearances, regulatory standing

## Philippines-First Launch

We're launching with focused support for **Philippine-licensed pilots**, leveraging Veremark's local infrastructure and our existing CAAP partnerships:

| Check Type | Coverage | Timeline |
|------------|----------|----------|
| CAAP License Verification | All PEL categories | 2-5 business days |
| Class 1/2 Medical | CAAP-accredited examiners | 1-3 business days |
| ATO Education | CAAP-approved institutions | 3-7 business days |
| NBI Clearance | Full Philippines coverage | 3-5 business days |

## The Verification Wallet

Verified credentials populate your **PilotRecognition Wallet** as cryptographically-signed W3C Verifiable Credentials:

- **Terminal 1 (Red)** — Revoked/expired credentials
- **Terminal 2 (Amber)** — Pending verification or isolated status
- **Terminal 3 (Green)** — Fully verified, exportable to any airline ATS

This three-tier system gives operators instant visual confirmation of credential status, eliminating the 6-week re-verification cycle that plagues pilot hiring today.
