---

## 5. Vesting Schedule

To protect both parties in the event of early departure:

- **Cliff:** 12 months from the date of this agreement — no equity vests before this point
- **Vesting period:** 36 months after the cliff (48 months total)
- **Monthly vesting:** 1/36th of total equity per month after cliff

**Example:** If Karl departs at month 18, he has completed 6 months of post-cliff vesting = 6/36 = ~16.7% of his 25% = approximately 4.2% vested.

Full equity vests at 48 months from agreement date.

## 9. Non-Compete

During the term of this agreement and for **12 months** following departure, neither party shall:
- Build or join a competing pilot credential or recognition platform
- Solicit PilotRecognition's clients, partners, or users for a competing service
- Use confidential information obtained through this venture for personal gain

This clause does not prevent either party from working in unrelated aviation roles (e.g., flying, instruction, operations).

---

## 10. Departure

### Voluntary Departure
A Co-Founder may exit by giving **30 days written notice**. Vested equity is retained. Unvested equity is forfeited and returns to the reserved pool.

### Involuntary Removal
A Co-Founder may be removed for cause (fraud, gross negligence, material breach of this agreement) by written agreement of the remaining Co-Founder. In this event, a **6-month buyout period** applies for vested equity at fair market value.

### Death or Incapacity
Vested equity passes to the Co-Founder's nominated estate. The remaining Co-Founder has a **right of first refusal** to purchase the equity at fair market value.

---

## 11. Future Investment

Upon raising external investment:
- Both Co-Founders' equity dilutes equally from the reserved pool first, then pro-rata
- Neither Co-Founder may accept investment on behalf of the venture without the other's written consent
- Any investor equity grants require a formal shareholders agreement superseding this document

---

## 12. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the **United Arab Emirates**, consistent with the operating entity's registration in the Dubai Freezone.

Disputes shall first be resolved through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, disputes shall be referred to mediation in Dubai before any legal proceedings.
