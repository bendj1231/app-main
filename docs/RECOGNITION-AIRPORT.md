→ "FAQ" — 6 questions with schema
→ CTA: "Join the Recognition Framework" (framework demo, not candidate browse)

**SEO Target:**
- Primary: `aviation recognition framework`, `pre-cleared pilot status`

**Critical Rule:** This page NEVER shows a list of pilots. Never says "browse candidates." Never implies searching profiles. It explains the FRAMEWORK and invites the airline to participate in the ecosystem.
