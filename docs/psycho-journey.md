## Writing Guidelines

### Tone
- Direct, no-nonsense
- Problem-first, not feature-first
- Avoid corporate jargon
- Use pilot language (hours, CPL, instructor line, pathways)

### Structure
- Journey narrative (start → middle → end)
- Stage-based progression (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum)
- Visual comparison (traditional vs new path)
- Specific outcomes (career results, not skills)

### Format
- Short, punchy headlines
- Minimal text per section
- Image-driven where possible
- Numbers for specificity (47 spots, 50 hours, 3 years)

### Voice
- "You" not "we"
- Active voice, not passive
- Specific examples, not general statements
- Real outcomes, not theoretical benefits
