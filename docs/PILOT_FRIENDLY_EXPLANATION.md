## The Glass Cockpit Analogy

**You know the screen in front of you when you fly?**

That digital display showing your attitude, altitude, and navigation — that's a **Glass Cockpit**.

**Here's the thing:** That screen doesn't actually **own** your engines. It doesn't **store** the fuel. It doesn't **keep** the radio towers inside it.

It simply **streams live data** from different parts of the airplane to your screen so you can see exactly what's happening in real-time.

**The moment you turn off the battery — that screen goes blank.** Nothing is saved on the glass itself.
