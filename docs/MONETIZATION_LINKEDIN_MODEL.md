# How PilotRecognition.com Can Adopt the LinkedIn Attestation Model

Aviation is heavily regulated. Verified pilot data carries significantly higher financial return than general corporate networking data. The core insight: **keep verification free for pilots, monetize trust indirectly through operators and third parties.**

---

## 1. Monetize the Enterprise "Operator Pull"

**The Funnel:**

1. **Free** — Enterprise posts public pathway cards. No cost. They see traction: pilot interest submissions, profile views, match counts.
2. **Traction creates buy-in** — they watch verified pilots engage with their pathway. The data is visible but not actionable without upgrading.
3. **Paid ($1,000/year)** — unlocks the full operator toolkit:
   - Pull API access to filter and contact verified pilots
   - Flight hours issuance — operator can formally endorse or verify hours for pilots they've trained or employed
   - Advanced filtering (type ratings, EBT score, hours band, nationality)
   - Priority placement in pilot-facing pathway rankings

**Referral Incentive (built into the subscription):**

- Enterprises recommend the platform to their own pilots (cadets, graduates, employed crew)
- **5% of any Recognition+ subscription** attributed to that enterprise goes back as account credit
- **$20 flat referral fee** per pilot who signs up and completes onboarding via the enterprise's referral link
- Net effect: enterprises actively market the platform to their own pilot pools at zero cost to PilotRecognition

**Industry Exposure:**

- Paid enterprise tier gets a verified operator badge, featured placement on pathway cards, and inclusion in the PilotRecognition operator directory
- Signals legitimacy in the pilot community — pilots trust pathways from verified operators over anonymous listings

**Value prop:** Post free, see pilots engage, pay to hire. The $1,000/year is not a cost — it's a profit subscription because the referral credits and hours issuance tool pay for themselves.

---

## 2. High-Margin Premium Subscription Tiers

**Strategy:** Offer basic verification free (e.g., matching a pilot to standard flight school data).

**Profit Mechanism:** `Recognition+` ($99/year) unlocks deep behavioral metrics:

- Automated gap analysis against complex airline pathway requirements
- Live-updating Recognition Score based on verified flight hours
- Enhanced visibility to recruiter algorithms
- Full credential issuance and wallet display

Free tier builds the database. Paid tier monetizes pilots who want to accelerate placement.

---

## 3. API Integrations with Aviation Platforms

**Strategy:** Become the single source of truth for pilot identity and competency.

**Profit Mechanism:** License a "Verified by PilotRecognition" API to third-party aviation systems:

- Crew management software (e.g., Jeppesen, Sabre, Lufthansa Systems)
- Simulator training centers (pre-verify candidate hours before sim booking)
- Scheduling platforms and rostering tools
- Aviation HR platforms and ATS systems

Pricing model: per-API-call or annual license. Reduces paperwork overhead for every platform that integrates.

---

## 4. Targeted Training & Insurance Marketplace Partnerships

**Strategy:** Use verified profile gaps to predict a pilot's career needs.

**Profit Mechanism:** When the platform identifies a gap (e.g., missing ATP, multi-engine rating, expired medical), surface targeted partner offers:

- Flight training organizations pay a lead-generation fee per referral
- Aviation insurance brokers pay for warm leads (pilots nearing license renewals)
- Type rating centers pay per qualified candidate referral

This is the aviation equivalent of LinkedIn's "Jobs You May Be Interested In" — except the targeting is based on verified credential gaps, not keyword matching.

---

## 5. Automated Regulatory Verification Pipelines

**Strategy:** Integrate directly with civil aviation authority databases (FAA Airmen Inquiry, ICAO ePL, CAAP, EASA).

**Profit Mechanism:** Abstract the painful manual process of foreign license validation:

- ICAO-to-FAA license conversion verification
- Foreign first officer transition packages for airlines
- Cadet program vetting at scale

Charge a processing fee per verification pipeline run. International cadet programs and airlines transitioning foreign pilots are the primary buyers.

## Platform Flywheel

```
Free pilot sign-ups
        ↓
Large verified database
        ↓
Operators pay to pull → Enterprise revenue
Pilots pay to stand out → Recognition+ revenue
Partners pay per referral → Marketplace revenue
Third parties pay per API call → Integration revenue
Regulators pay for pipeline access → Verification revenue
```

The free layer is not a cost centre — it is the asset. Every unverified pilot who joins and completes onboarding increases the value of the database for every paying operator on the other side.
