## Executive Summary

This is not a job board. This is not a data broker. This is a **neutral, stateless protocol** that shifts how the aviation industry trusts data.

By refusing to own, store, or monetize pilot credentials, we remove the target from our back and become the "Switzerland of Aviation" — a cryptographic pipeline where suspicious competitors can execute safe handshakes without exposing secrets.

## The 5% Activation Credit (Not Escrow)

### The Critical Reframe

**OLD (Risky — Extortion Narrative):**
> "We have your $5. Pay $1,000 in 5 days or we take it back."

**NEW (Clean — Elite Privilege):**
> "You generated a $5 Member Credit. Join Enterprise within 5 days to claim it as a discount."

### Why This Works

| Aspect | Old Approach | New Approach |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Feeling** | Extortion, heavy-handed | Elite corporate perk |
| **Legality** | Complex escrow accounting | Standard promotional rewards |
| **Verification** | Blocked if not paid | Proceeds regardless |
| **PR Risk** | "Weaponizing our records" | "Missed optimization opportunity" |
| **Outcome** | Adversarial | Value-driven |

### The Flow

1. **Verification Event** — Pilot initiates, ATO responds, triangulation succeeds
2. **Credit Generated** — System calculates 5% ($5) as "New Member Activation Credit"
3. **Notification** — ATO receives: "$5 credit reserved — activate Enterprise Seat to claim"
4. **5-Day Window** — Countdown to subscribe and apply credit as discount
5. **Grace Period Expires** — Credit "lapses" (returns to platform) — clean language

**Cash flow identical. Narrative flipped. Neutrality preserved.**

## The Four Unforeseen Risks & Mitigations

### 1. The Protection Racket PR Trap (PATCHED)
**Risk:** ATOs feel extorted by 5-day "hostage" deadline  
**Patch:** Reframed as "New Member Activation Credit" — promotional benefit, not penalty

### 2. The $5 Rubber-Stamping Incentive (PATCHED)
**Risk:** ATO clerks automate approvals for easy money  
**Patch:** Cryptographic triangulation — no human "Approve" button, only hash matching

### 3. The CAA Data Desert (PATCHED)
**Risk:** Third-world registries lack APIs  
**Patch:** Decoupled verification — Veremark handles qualifications, Flight Directive handles hours separately

### 4. The 24-Hour Crew Rest Problem (NEEDS WORK)
**Risk:** Pilots miss opportunities during mandatory rest  
**Patch:** "Crew Rest Aware Windows" — extend to 72 hours if pilot in-flight or within 12hrs of landing

### 5. The Stateless Legal Illusion (NEEDS WORK)
**Risk:** GDPR still classifies display as "processing"  
**Patch:** 
- One-Hour Ephemeral Window (auto-purge after 60 min)
- Operational justification logging (flight number, vacancy ID)
- Pilot kill-switch (instant revoke authorization)
