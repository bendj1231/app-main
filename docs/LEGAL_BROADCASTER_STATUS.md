## The Legal Separation: Host vs. Broadcaster

## Terms of Service: Stateless Intermediary Clause

### Draft Legal Language

```
SECTION 14: STATELESS INTERMEDIARY & MERE CONDUIT STATUS

14.1 Classification of Service

PilotRecognition.com operates exclusively as a stateless digital broadcaster 
and mere conduit intermediary. We do not:

(a) Store, host, or retain personal data on our servers;
(b) Control, verify, or guarantee the accuracy of third-party data;
(c) Act as a data broker, data processor, or data controller under GDPR, 
    the Philippine Data Privacy Act, or equivalent regulations;
(d) Maintain databases of pilot credentials, flight logs, or registry 
    documents.

14.2 Broadcast Function

Our platform functions as a neutral transmission layer, temporarily 
projecting encrypted data streams from third-party verification 
providers (Veremark) to authenticated viewers at the explicit 
cryptographic consent of the data subject (pilot).

Data transmission is:
• Ephemeral (session-based only)
• Client-side rendered (browser memory)
• Zero-retention (no server-side storage)
• Consent-driven (pilot authorization required)

14.3 Liability Limitation

As a mere conduit intermediary, PilotRecognition.com bears ZERO 
liability for:

• Data accuracy or completeness
• Third-party verification errors
• Actions taken by viewers based on broadcast data
• Privacy breaches occurring outside our transmission layer
• Regulatory compliance of data originators

All legal, financial, and compliance liability rests with:
• Veremark (the verification processor)
• Data origin nodes (CAE, ForeFlight, registries)
• Viewing parties (airlines, operators)

14.4 Legal Defenses

In any dispute, investigation, or legal proceeding, we assert:

1. NEUTRAL BROADCASTER status under international intermediary laws;
2. MERE CONDUIT protection under the Philippine Data Privacy Act 
   (Section 4, Intermediary Liability) and EU E-Commerce Directive 
   (Article 12-14, Mere Conduit);
3. ZERO POSSESSION defense — we do not "possess" personal data 
   as defined under applicable privacy regulations;
4. CONSENT-DRIVEN architecture — all broadcasts require explicit 
   cryptographic authorization from the data subject.

14.5 Third-Party Accountability

Users acknowledge that PilotRecognition.com is a stateless routing 
layer only. All data verification, accuracy guarantees, and compliance 
responsibilities rest exclusively with our third-party infrastructure 
partners, specifically:

• VEREMARK (verification execution)
• AUTH0 (identity and encryption)
• Data Origin Nodes (source of truth)
```
