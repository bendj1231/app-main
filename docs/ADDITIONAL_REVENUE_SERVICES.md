# Additional Revenue Services
## Five High-Margin Micro-Services

**Date:** May 19, 2026

---

## Service 1: Dynamic Crew-Risk Telemetry

**Payer:** Pillar 23 — Aviation Insurance Underwriters / Lloyd's Syndicates

**Problem:** Insurers price fleet policies using static data, no real-time crew risk visibility.

**Service:** Anonymized risk-aggregation API. Tracks compliance across Pillar 13 (Aeromedical) and Pillar 6 (Type Ratings). Exposes aggregated operator safety score — NOT personal names or individual logs.

**Revenue:** Continuous API access fee. Syndicates integrate into pricing models, dynamically adjust premiums.

---

## Service 2: EBT Telemetry Pipes

**Payers:** Pillar 18 (ATOs like CAE) & Pillar 1 (Commercial Airlines)

**Problem:** Simulator data stranded in training centers, real-world telemetry stranded in airline FDM software. No connection for Evidence-Based Training.

**Service:** Stateless data bridge mapping Pillar 15 (OEMs) flight data to Type Rating Center ingest engines.

**Revenue:** Per-session data-routing fee. Removes manual overhead, aligns training with operational metrics.

---

## Service 3: Remote ID & Autonomous Operator Auth

**Payer:** Pillar 4 — Advanced Air Mobility / eVTOL / Drone Networks

**Problem:** Ground control must instantly prove remote operator holds valid license for specific drone category.

**Service:** Machine-to-machine Remote Pilot Identity Verification API. Token through gateway pings sovereign registry (FAA DroneZone, CAAs), returns cryptographic confirmation.

**Revenue:** Fraction-of-a-cent per automated flight plan clearance. High volume = massive aggregate revenue.

---

## Service 4: Ready-to-Deploy Talent Bidding

**Payers:** Aviation Recruitment Agencies & Pillar 3 (Charter/Business Aviation)

**Problem:** Sudden crew shortages take weeks of manual vetting to find qualified pilots.

**Service:** Blind, compliance-filtered talent pipeline. Query by verified parameters: "B737 rated, 1,500 hours, current medical, legally clear today."

**Revenue:** Substantial per-placement booking fee. Broker pays for pre-audited, instantly deployable talent.

---

## Service 5: Credit-Risk & Asset Lease Validation

**Payers:** Pillar 8 (Banks) & Credit Rating Agencies

**Problem:** Financing $100M hull or pilot training loans, no efficient way to evaluate professional consistency or employment stability.

**Service:** Professional Standing Verification API. With pilot consent, banks query Pillar 12 (Academic) and Employment History Nodes during underwriting.

**Revenue:** Heavy premium per credit/asset report. Removes manual auditing, lowers default rates.
