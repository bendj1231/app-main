## The Risk (Be Smart)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Becomes a complaints forum** | Keep the tone solution-oriented. "I left because..." → "Here's what I need to come back." Every testimony ends with a forward path, not a dead end. |
| **Airline backlash** | Airlines are your customers too. Frame it as: "We fix the pipeline so airlines get pre-verified pilots faster." The testimonies expose the *gap*, not the airline. |
| **ALPA / media pushback** | The verification system protects you. These aren't opinions. These are CFI-signed, CAA-validated records. The data speaks. You don't have to. |

### The Anonymous Advantage

> "You can testify your story anonymously. The industry still verifies it. The claim is still real. You just choose how much of yourself you expose."

**Expect 30-40% anonymity in the first wave.** This is not a bug. It's a feature. It proves pilots feel safe. It proves the stories are real. It proves the fear of retaliation is so high that even a verified, anonymous platform is the only safe way to speak.

### The Verification Bottleneck

| Week | Verified / Submitted | Bottleneck |
|------|---------------------|------------|
| 1 | 10 / 50 | Manual review speed |
| 2 | 35 / 120 | ATO document turnaround |
| 3 | 60 / 200 | OCR pre-screening kicks in |
| 4 | 100 / 300 | Human review + automated pipeline |

**Hire one reviewer at Week 2 if submission rate exceeds 100/week.**
