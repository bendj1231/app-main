## The Warning: Lessons from Rome's Fall

**What Killed Rome (What Could Kill Us):**

1. **Overexpansion (Trying to conquer everything at once)**
   - Focus: Aviation only. No maritime. No rail.
   - Master one vertical before adjacent markets.

2. **Debasement of Currency (Giving away too much)**
   - Free tier is acquisition, not business model
   - Must convert 15%+ to paid within 18 months

3. **Reliance on Mercenaries (Outsourcing core)**
   - Verification is core — never fully outsource
   - Veremark is partner, not replacement

4. **Barbarian Invasions (New entrants)**
   - Build moat: data + network effects
   - Watch for: LinkedIn entering aviation, ICAO building API

5. **Political Instability (Founder drama)**
   - Equity split finalized in year 1
   - Clear CEO, clear succession plan
   - No co-CEO experiments
