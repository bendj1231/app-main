\section{The Founder's Narrative}

\subsection{The Genesis Story}
This platform was born from lived experience. As a pilot who invested \$50,000 USD to earn a commercial license, I was labeled ``low-timer'' and ``not fit for the job''---all because I had 200 hours instead of 1,500.

\subsection{The Career Fair Revelation}
At a major Aviation Career Fair, approaching as a pilot: ``You know the requirements. 1,500 hours.'' Approaching as a developer: welcoming, engaged. The industry prioritizes infrastructure over advocacy---yet the two are inseparable.

\subsection{The Breaking Point}
A graduate from an aviation family invested 6 million PHP (\$100,000 USD) in an ATR type rating and received no placement. If someone with industry connections cannot navigate the system, what chance does the average person have?

\subsection{The Swiss Cheese Model}
\begin{mdframed}[linecolor=prNavy, linewidth=1pt, backgroundcolor=prGray]
\textbf{Layer 1:} Bored captains don't move $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Layer 2:} First Officers cannot upgrade $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Layer 3:} Airlines don't hire instructors $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Layer 4:} Instructors stay trapped $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Layer 5:} Entry-level jobs never open $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Layer 6:} 2015 graduates still waiting
\end{mdframed}

\section{Hub A: Operations \& Recruitment}

\subsection{Pillar 1: Commercial Airlines}
\textbf{Requirements:} Publish Pathways with exact prerequisites. Use Profile Pull dashboard.

\textbf{Pricing:} Basic \$500/mo, Professional \$1,000/mo, Enterprise \$2,500/mo. Success fee \$500/hire.

\textbf{Benefits:} 70\% time-to-hire reduction, \$1.2-2.4M annual ROI.

\subsection{Pillar 2: Cargo Operators}
\textbf{Requirements:} CBTA cargo focus dimensions. Segregated talent pool.

\textbf{Benefits:} Washout 50\%$\rightarrow$20\%, ROI \$1.8-2.2M.

\subsection{Pillar 3: Charter \& BizAv}
\textbf{Requirements:} Stealth Pathways, 30-day deployment, enhanced security.

\textbf{Benefits:} 90 days$\rightarrow$14 days, \$8,500 vs \$25,000 recruitment cost.

\subsection{Pillar 4: Emerging Sectors}
EVTOL, Air Taxi, Agriculture pathways. 40-60\% training cost reduction.

\section{Hub B: Training \& Transition}

\subsection{Pillar 5: Flight Schools (ATOs)}

\textbf{Pricing:} \$200-1,000/mo + \$20/referral.

\textbf{Benefits:} 30\% enrollment increase, market differentiation.
