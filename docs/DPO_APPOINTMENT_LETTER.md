## 1. Appointment

I, Marie Maureen Synthia Maya, in my capacity as Managing Director of Aviation Pathways Ltd (the "Company"), hereby appoint **Benjamin Bowler** as the **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** of the Company, effective from the date first written above.

## 2. Scope of Appointment

This appointment is made pursuant to Section 57(2)(b) of the Data Protection Act 2017 of the Republic of Mauritius, which permits a body corporate to designate a person or persons to be responsible for ensuring compliance with the Act.

## 3. Responsibilities

As Data Protection Officer, Benjamin Bowler shall:

1. **Monitor compliance** with the Data Protection Act 2017 and any subsidiary legislation made thereunder;
2. **Develop and maintain** the Company's data protection policies, privacy policies, and procedures for handling personal data;
3. **Oversee** the Company's Register of Processing Activities and ensure it is accurate and up to date;
4. **Assess and document** the lawfulness of all personal data processing activities undertaken by the Company;
5. **Respond to** data subject access requests, rectification requests, erasure requests, and objections in a timely manner and in accordance with the Act;
6. **Act as the primary point of contact** between the Company and the Data Protection Office of the Republic of Mauritius;
7. **Notify the Data Protection Office** within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any personal data breach;
8. **Advise the directors** on data protection impact assessments (DPIAs) where required;
9. **Ensure** that data protection considerations are embedded into the design and operation of the Company's products, services, and internal processes (privacy by design);
10. **Maintain records** of all data processing activities as required under Section 57(3) of the Act;
11. **Provide training and guidance** to any employees, contractors, or agents of the Company on data protection obligations;
12. **Review and update** the Company's Data Breach Response Plan (PR-BRP-001) on at least an annual basis.

## 4. Authority

In the discharge of his duties, the DPO is authorised to:

- Access all personal data, processing records, and technical systems of the Company;
- Request information from any employee, contractor, or director relevant to data protection compliance;
- Report directly to the Managing Director and, where necessary, to the Board of Directors on matters relating to data protection;
- Engage external legal, technical, or compliance advisors at the Company's expense, subject to prior approval of the Managing Director for costs exceeding MUR 5,000;
- Represent the Company in correspondence with the Data Protection Office;
- Make recommendations to the Board on changes to data protection practices, policies, or infrastructure.

## 5. Independence

The DPO shall perform his duties independently and shall not receive instructions from the Board or any director on the exercise of his functions under this appointment. The DPO shall not be dismissed or penalised for performing his duties in accordance with this appointment and the Act. The DPO may be removed only by ordinary resolution of the sole shareholder in accordance with the procedures set out in the Company's Articles of Association.

## 6. Resources

The Company undertakes to provide the DPO with:

- Adequate time to discharge his responsibilities (estimated at not less than five (5) hours per week);
- Access to legal and technical resources necessary for compliance monitoring;
- Sufficient budget for training, certification, and external advice;
- Direct reporting line to the Managing Director without interference.

## 8. Term

This appointment shall continue unless and until:

- (a) The DPO resigns by giving not less than thirty (30) days' written notice to the Managing Director;
- (b) The DPO is removed by ordinary resolution of the sole shareholder in accordance with the Articles of Association;
- (c) The DPO ceases to be a director of the Company;
- (d) The Company is wound up or dissolved.

## 9. Governing Law

This appointment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Mauritius, including the Data Protection Act 2017 and any regulations made thereunder.
