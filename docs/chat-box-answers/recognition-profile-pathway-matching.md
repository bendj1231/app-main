## 📝 Full Version (for a richer chat reply or expandable card)

Great question — and this is where PilotRecognition really differs from a job board. Instead of you applying blindly and hoping for the best, the platform **continuously compares your live Recognition Profile against every active Career Pathway** and tells you exactly where you stand.

### 🧩 What the Recognition Profile Contains
Your profile isn't just a CV — it's a live, structured record of your aviation identity:
- **Flight hours** broken down by category (PIC, multi-engine, turbine, cross-country, night, instrument)
- **Type ratings** and aircraft-specific experience
- **Medical class** and recency status
- **License validity** across jurisdictions
- **EBT/CBTA readiness**
- **Background check** status (Veremark)
- **Nationality & right-to-work** for visa-sensitive roles
- **Recognition Score** — the composite metric airlines filter by

### 🎯 What a Career Pathway Contains
Every pathway is a **structured Pathway Card** published by an operator. Instead of vague job posts saying "competitive experience required," each card lists exactly:
- Required hours by type (e.g. *1,500 hrs, 500 multi-engine PIC*)
- Type ratings needed
- Medical class required
- EBT/CBTA expectations
- Nationality eligibility (e.g. EU only, sponsorship available, etc.)
- Recognition Score threshold
- Preferred endorsements and competencies

### ⚙️ How the Match Works
The pathway matching engine runs a real-time comparison every time a pathway is published or your profile changes:

| Match Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Match** | You meet every requirement. Submit interest with one click. |
| 🟡 **Partial Match** | You're close. The engine shows you **exactly what's missing**. |
| 🔴 **Gap** | Significant criteria unmet. The engine shows the **structured pathway to close the gap** (specific type rating, additional hours, EBT assessment, etc.). |

For a partial match, the system doesn't just stop at "you're not qualified." It generates a **recommended development route** — including partner ATOs where you can complete the missing training, often at a discount for Recognition+ members.

### ⭐ Priority Matching (Recognition+)
Free members get **2 pathway submissions and 3 profile comparisons per month**. Recognition+ members get:
- **Unlimited** pathway submissions
- **Unlimited** profile comparisons
- **AI-ranked priority placement** in airline search results
- **Operator notifications** when a new pathway matches your profile
- **Fast-track interviews** during hiring surges
- **EBT/CBTA queue-skipping** after Foundation training

### 🔁 Continuous Circulation
The match isn't a one-time event. When you log new hours, earn a type rating, or your medical renews, **your profile is re-matched instantly** against every pathway you previously didn't qualify for. Pathways you couldn't reach yesterday become open today — and you'll be the first to know.

🎯 **The bottom line:** Your Recognition Profile turns Career Pathways from a guessing game into a **live, gap-aware, AI-ranked matching system** — showing you exactly where you fit, where you don't, and the fastest route to get there.
