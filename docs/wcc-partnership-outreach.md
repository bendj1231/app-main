## What PilotRecognition Does for Your Graduates

- **Verified credential wallet** — pilots store their CAAP license, medical certificate, ratings, and training records in a cryptographic wallet they own and control
- **Pathway cards** — specific, transparent requirements posted by airlines and operators so graduates know exactly what to build toward
- **Recognition profile** — a live, real-time profile that updates as pilots log hours, complete programs, and earn endorsements
- **Atlas Aviation CV** — a structured, airline-ready profile format that replaces the PDF resume

Your graduates stop being invisible. They become findable, verifiable, and aligned.
