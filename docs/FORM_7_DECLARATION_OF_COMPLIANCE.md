# LEGAL CONSEQUENCES OF FALSE DECLARATION

Under the **Companies Act 2001**, a person who makes a false declaration under Section 13 commits an offence and is liable on conviction to:

- **A fine** not exceeding MUR 100,000
- **Imprisonment** for a term not exceeding 5 years
- **Both** fine and imprisonment
