# PilotRecognition Brand Messaging Guide
## "We Are NOT a Job Board"

---

## The Core Message

**What We Are:**
A pilot recognition and information platform. Pilots get verified, discover pathways, and access information. No job promises. No applications. Just recognition and discovery.

**What We Are NOT:**
A job board. We don't post jobs. We don't accept applications. We don't promise employment.

---

## What Each Pillar Actually Contributes

### Hub A: Operations (Pillars 1-4)
**What they contribute:** Pathway information, fleet data, operational requirements
**What they get:** Visibility to verified pilots who match their needs
**NOT:** A hiring platform. Just discovery and recognition.

### Hub B: Training (Pillars 5-7)
**What they contribute:** Training pathway information, type rating data
**What they get:** Graduates who are pre-verified and recognition-ready
**NOT:** A recruitment pipeline. Just information and verification.

### Hub C: Capital (Pillars 8-9)
**What they contribute:** Financial product information, risk assessment data
**What they get:** Verified pilot profiles for accurate underwriting
**NOT:** Job placement. Just verified data.

### Hub D: Infrastructure (Pillars 11-13)
**What they contribute:** Verification systems, data feeds, medical tracking
**What they get:** Integrated recognition layer
**NOT:** Employment services. Just infrastructure.

### Hub E: Community (Pillars 14-15)
**What they contribute:** Knowledge sharing, mentorship networks
**What they get:** Access to recognized pilots seeking guidance
**NOT:** Job referrals. Just community.

### Hub F: Growth (Pillars 16-20)
**What they contribute:** Event information, media, regulatory updates
**What they get:** Connection to recognized pilot community
**NOT:** Career placement. Just information.

### Hub G: Discovery (Pillar 25)
**What they contribute:** Search and discovery platforms
**What they get:** Structured recognition data instead of scattered listings
**NOT:** Job aggregation. Just discovery.

## The Freemium Model (For Pilots)

**Free Tier:**
- See what pathways exist
- Basic Recognition Score
- Browse type rating information
- View manufacturer data

**Recognition Plus ($99/year):**
- Full profile verification
- Detailed pathway comparison
- Recognition score breakdown
- Priority visibility to stakeholders

**What This Means:**
- Free = "See what's out there"
- Paid = "Get verified and recognized"
- Never = "Apply for jobs"

## The Three Pathway Types

### 1. Airline/Cargo Pathways
**What pilots see:** Requirements, fleet information, operational details
**What they DON'T see:** Job openings, application buttons, hiring guarantees

### 2. Manufacturer Type Ratings
**What pilots see:** Aircraft specifications, type rating costs, training paths
**What they DON'T see:** Job promises, employment commitments

### 3. Specialized/Alternative Pathways
**What pilots see:** Corporate, eVTOL, charter information
**What they DON'T see:** Job listings, hiring announcements

## Pull vs Push (Critical Distinction)

**Job Boards (Push):**
- Pilots push resumes
- Employers post jobs
- Active application process
- Promise of employment

**PilotRecognition (Pull):**
- Pilots get recognized (verified)
- Stakeholders pull data
- Passive discovery model
- No employment promises
