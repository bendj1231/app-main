## 1. Executive Summary

Mauritius offers an optimal jurisdiction for PilotRecognition's international aviation technology platform through:

- **0% capital gains tax** on intellectual property and equity sales
- **15% corporate tax rate** (effective 3% with tax credits)
- **No withholding tax** on dividends to non-residents
- **Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAAs)** with 46 countries including Philippines, UAE, Singapore
- **IP holding company regime** for software/technology assets
- **Access to African markets** through AfCFTA and COMESA

- Names must include "Ltd" or "Limited"
- Cannot conflict with existing trademarks

## 5. Tax Framework & Optimization

### Corporate Tax Structure

```
Standard Rate: 15%
Effective Rate: 3% (with foreign tax credit)

Tax Calculation Example (Year 1):
Gross Revenue:        $2,000,000
Operating Expenses:  ($1,200,000)
————————————————————————————————
Net Taxable Income:     $800,000
Tax @ 15%:            ($120,000)

Foreign Tax Credit:    +$96,000
(80% deemed paid on foreign income)
————————————————————————————————
Net Tax Payable:         $24,000  (3% effective)
```

### Intellectual Property Regime

**Patent Box / IP Holding Benefits:**
- **80% exemption** on IP-derived income
- Qualifying IP: EBT video scoring algorithm, pathway matching engine, pilot verification system
- Effective tax rate on IP income: **3%**

**IP Holding Structure:**
```
PilotRecognition Ltd (Mauritius)
├── Owns: EBT Scoring Algorithm Patent
├── Owns: Pathway Matching Engine Copyright
├── Owns: PilotRecognition Trademark Portfolio
├── Licenses to: Operating subsidiaries (Philippines, UAE, Singapore)
└── Royalties: Tax-advantaged under Mauritius IP regime
```

### Value Added Tax (VAT)

- **Registration threshold:** MUR 6 million (~$130,000 USD) annually
- **Standard rate:** 15%
- **Exempt services:** Educational services (Foundation/Transition programs)
- **Zero-rated:** Export of SaaS services to non-residents

**Recommendation:** Voluntary VAT registration to reclaim input VAT on professional services

## 8. Philippines Subsidiary (CAAP Operations)

### Structure Options

**Option A: Branch Office (Simpler)**
- Mauritius company registers with Philippine SEC
- No separate legal entity
- Full liability exposure to parent
- Local hiring: Work permits required

**Option B: 100% Domestic Subsidiary (Recommended)**
- Philippine Inc. (Domestic Corporation)
- Mauritius parent owns 100%
- Limited liability ring-fence
- Easier local partnerships (ATO agreements)

### Philippine Requirements

| Step | Agency | Timeline | Cost |
|------|--------|----------|------|
| SEC Name Verification | Securities & Exchange Commission | 1 day | ₱100 |
| Articles of Incorporation | SEC | 5-7 days | ₱5,000+ |
| BIR Registration | Bureau of Internal Revenue | 7 days | ₱500 |
| Business Permit | Local government (Pampanga/Binalonan) | 3-5 days | ₱5,000 |
| SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG | Social security agencies | 7 days | Free |

**Minimum Capital:** ₱5,000 (domestic corporation)
**Directors:** Minimum 5 (can be 100% foreign for non-restricted activities)
